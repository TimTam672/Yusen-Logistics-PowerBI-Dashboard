### 🚀 Key Features & Engineering Highlights

#### 🔒 Data Governance & Compliance
* **Data Compliance:** All source data was systematically anonymized using dummy department names and values to strictly comply with corporate confidentiality policies.

#### 📊 Strategic Financial & Resource Auditing
* **Financial Health Audit:** Engineered a dual-axis matrix overlaying Gross Profit (GP) with Selling, General, and Administrative (SGA) lines to intercept spending anomalies during low-GP periods, tracking rolling Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) metrics for proactive headcount management.
* **Hierarchical SGA Auditing:** Designed a multi-layered SGA Treemap with button parameters to toggle between absolute values and proportions, enabling interactive drill-down from core corporate levels down to micro-divisions to isolate overspending.
* **Target & Expense Controls:** Built dynamic variance tables and sub-ledger "Top 3 Gap" matrices to regulate overspending. Cross-examined full-year budgets against Year-to-Date (YTD) actuals to monitor progress toward annual milestones, dynamically adjusting logic across sub-ledgers (e.g., *Expected vs. Actual Revenue* for income accounts; *Budget vs. Actual Spending* for expense lines).
* **Cost Decay & Forecasting:** Integrated a financial Funnel Chart mapping the complete margin breakdown (Sales Revenue down to Operating Profit 3 / OP3) alongside synchronized pie/donut charts to monitor SGA encroachment on OP2. Structured side-by-side Forecast vs. Actual consoles for continuous variance tracking.

#### 📈 Operational Capacity & Cross-Department Efficiency
* **Capacity Optimization:** Formulated 6-month rolling average line charts for Air and Ocean divisions to eliminate seasonal noise. Paired these with latest-month shipments-per-FTE metrics to audit labor efficiency.
* **Cross-Department Resource Tracking:** Tailored structural analytics for the 4 core business segments (**ECO, RFD, WLD, FMD**) and their aggregation (**YLHK**). Modeled resource-sharing workflows to track how main divisions borrowed human resources from smaller support departments (**CSD, MTD**), ensuring accurate cost allocation.

#### 🛠️ Power BI Engineering & Interactive Architecture
* **Advanced DAX & Filtering:** Utilized complex DAX measures for context-aware data filtering, multi-scenario budget calculations, and dynamic ranking within sub-ledgers.
* **Dynamic UI/UX Architecture:** Leveraged Page Navigators, Bookmarks, and Slicers to enable fluid, parameter-driven switching between alternative dates, interactive charts, and diverse display options without cluttered layouts.
